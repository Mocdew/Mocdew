# Hi, I'm Olumide 👋

Data scientist building end-to-end ML systems — from raw data to deployed dashboards — with a focus on **credit risk, financial time series, and forecasting**. Also an avid VALORANT/esports fan who enjoys turning competitive gameplay data into analytical insight.

## 🔭 What I'm working on

- **[Intelligent Loan Underwriting & Default Risk Prediction Engine](https://github.com/Mocdew/Intelligent-Loan-Underwriting-Default-Risk-Prediction-Engine)** — end-to-end underwriting platform predicting default risk on 255K+ records with Logistic Regression & LightGBM, plus a real-time what-if simulator and interactive risk dashboard.
- **[CrediPulse — Explainable Credit Scoring Platform](https://github.com/Mocdew/CrediPulse-Explainable-Credit-Scoring-Platform)** — credit scoring with a focus on model explainability.
- **[Brent Crude Oil Price & Volatility Forecasting](https://github.com/Mocdew/Brent-Crude-Oil-Price-Volatility-Forecasting)** — four decades of oil price data, benchmarking ARIMA/GARCH against gradient boosting.
- **[EUR/USD Volatility Forecasting with Deep Learning](https://github.com/Mocdew/EUR-USD-Volatility-Forecasting-with-Deep-Learning)** — applying neural sequence models to FX volatility.
- **[CPI Inflation Forecasting](https://github.com/Mocdew/CPI-Inflation-Forecasting)** — macroeconomic time series forecasting.
