## SELECTED WORK

<br>

**[recoup](https://github.com/Mocdew/Bach)**
`Bach`
Failed-payment recovery timing for subscription merchants. A mixture-cure hazard model separates *"gone for good"* from *"not paid yet"*, an exact schedule planner values entire retry sequences rather than single guesses, and a cross-fitted off-policy gate blocks deployment until the lift is statistically proven — not simulated. Benchmarked at **+35% recovered revenue per failed invoice** over a fixed retry ladder, holding across seven perturbed simulation scenarios.
<br>`Python` · `scipy` · `hierarchical models` · `Thompson sampling` · `off-policy evaluation`

<br>

**[Intelligent Loan Underwriting & Default Risk Prediction Engine](https://github.com/Mocdew/Intelligent-Loan-Underwriting-Default-Risk-Prediction-Engine)**
End-to-end underwriting platform predicting default risk across **255K+ records**, paired with a real-time what-if simulator and an interactive risk dashboard.
<br>`Logistic Regression` · `LightGBM` · `Streamlit`

<br>

**[CrediPulse](https://github.com/Mocdew/CrediPulse-Explainable-Credit-Scoring-Platform)**
